### Macau Chess Open Games Archive
This is an **unofficial** archive of the games in the Macau Chess Open. We made this archive in 2026, and it is maintained by players in the tournament.
This repository is also used for Lichess Broadcasts.

#### General information:
- The Macau Open started in 1984 and has been held annually ever since.
- It is a non-FIDE tournament; the time control is 90+30 standard.
- It was organized by José Silveirinha (former Macau chess president from 1991-2015) between 1984-1986, 1989-1998 and 2000-2015; Fernado Lima between 1987 and 1988; Paul Chan in 1999 and by Herman Ho from 2016-now.
- Every 2 years, the top 5 finishers of the Macau Chess Open will represent Macau in the FIDE Chess Olympiad.

#### Some things to note:
- Forfeited games are not archived.
- We do not have clock nor elo data.
- Some scoresheets had missing or illegal moves - we would try our best to restore the games. If our attempts fail, the game would not be added in the archive.
# Macau Chess Open Games Archive

This is an unofficial archive of games from the Macau Chess Open. This archive was created in 2026 and is maintained by tournament players. This repository is also used for Lichess Broadcasts.

## General Information

* **History:** The Macau Open started in 1984 and has been held annually ever since.
* **Format & Time Control:** It is a non-FIDE tournament with a standard time control of 90+30.
* **FIDE Olympiad Selection:** Every 2 years, the top 5 finishers of the Macau Chess Open represent Macau in the FIDE Chess Olympiad.

### Tournament Organizers
* **1984–1986, 1989–1998, 2000–2015:** José Silveirinha (former Macau Chess President from 1991–2015)
* **1987–1988:** Fernando Lima
* **1999:** Paul Chan
* **2016–present:** Herman Ho

## Important Notes

* **Forfeited Games:** Forfeited games are not archived.
* **Data Limitations:** Clock times and Elo data are not available.
* **Damaged Scoresheets:** Some historical scoresheets contain missing or illegal moves. We try our best to reconstruct and restore these games. If a game cannot be accurately restored, it is excluded from the archive.
