# 2026 Macau Chess Open Games Archive

This is an unofficial, personal-maintained digital archive of games from the **2026 Macau Chess Open**. Established in 2026 by tournament players, this repository serves as a permanent database for past games and acts as the data hub for live **Lichess Broadcasts**.

---

## 🏆 General Information

### 📜 Tournament History & Format
* **Est. 1984:** The Macau Open was founded in 1984 and has been held annually as Macau's premier chess event.
* **Format & Time Control:** Hosted as a standard open tournament with a standard time control of **90 minutes + 30-second increment** per move.
* **Olympiad Selection Hub:** Every two years, this tournament serves as the official qualifying stage. The **top 5 finishers** earn the right to represent Macau on the international stage at the **FIDE Chess Olympiad**.

## ⚠️ Data & Archive Integrity Notices

To ensure the historical reliability of this database, please keep the following data limitations and archive boundaries in mind:

* 🚫 **Forfeited Games:** Games decided by forfeit or abandonment are omitted from this archive, as no playable chess data was generated.
* 🕒 **Clock & Rating Data:** Due to data source limitations, historical clock times and live Elo rating data are unavailable within these PGNs.
* 📝 **Damaged Scoresheets & Reconstruction:** 
  Some historical or handwritten scoresheets contain illegible text, missing notations, or illegal moves. Our team makes every effort to cross-reference and reconstruct these games accurately. If a game cannot be reliably verified or restored, **it is excluded from the collection** to maintain perfect data integrity. 

> 🗒️ **Note:** Any excluded or unrecoverable games will be logged for public reference in the [missing_games_log.md](./Games%20and%20Information/missing_games_log.md) file.
